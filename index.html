About Zero Barrier

We believe everyone deserves access to employment opportunities, regardless of their technology access. Zero Barrier bridges the digital divide by connecting low-tech workers with local employers through simple SMS technology.

Our platform removes barriers like smartphone requirements, internet access, and digital literacy, making job searching accessible to everyone in rural and urban communities.
